# 📖 Adam's Book of Energy
**By Author Adam (Age 9)**

---

## 🌟 Page 1: What Are Energy and Power?
Energy is like an invisible superpower inside everything in the universe! 
- **Energy** is what makes things move, light up, warm up, and grow.
- **Power** is how fast we use or make that energy!
- **Cool Science Rule**: Energy can never be destroyed—it just changes from one form into another!

---

## ☀️ Page 2: Chapter 1 — Power from the Sun (Solar Energy)
Every day, the sun sends giant beams of light and heat to Earth and through outer space.
- **Solar Panels** catch sunlight and turn it straight into electricity.
- Astronauts use solar wings on space stations and satellites to get power while flying in space!

---

## 💨🌊 Page 3: Chapter 2 — Wind & Underwater Power
Nature is always moving, and we can catch that movement to make clean electricity!
- **Wind Power**: When the wind blows, it spins giant white wind turbine blades.
- **Hydro (Water) Power**: Rushing rivers and deep ocean waves spin underwater wheels called turbines.

---

## 🍕🏃 Page 4: Chapter 3 — Pizza & Running Power!
Did you know your body is like a living power station?
- **Stored Energy**: Yummy food like **pizza** and **ice cream** holds stored energy inside it.
- **Motion Energy**: When you eat, your body turns that food into fuel so your muscles can **run super fast** and play with Lego!

---

## 🚀🪐 Page 5: Chapter 4 — Rocket Power to Saturn!
To travel into outer space and visit **Saturn's icy rings**, astronauts need mega power!
- Rockets mix fuel and oxygen to create a giant blast of hot gas that pushes the rocket up into the sky.
- Once in space, solar panels and super batteries keep the spaceship running all the way to Saturn!
